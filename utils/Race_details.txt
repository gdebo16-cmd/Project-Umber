# Eldridia — Ancestry Look Locks (v1)

For class/subclass sheets. Canon first; soft defaults only where chronicle is thin.

| Ancestry | Build / stature | Face & features | Palette / cues | Notes |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------|----------------|-------|
| **Human** | Average Lys midland | Varied; warm skin tones common | Ordinary cloth/steel | Default for earlier class sheets |
| **Uron** | **Bear-like** minotaur-kin (not bull), **6–7 ft** typical | Broad snout, thick fur, large fangs possible | Earth browns, black coats, jungle Caldera | Noth = Lys-pledged Uron; Shai = Caldera Uron |
| **Umber Elf** | Human-average height, rugged | **Long ears**; **red eyes** (canon lock; purple retired for defaults); mountain-hard faces | Snow-peak cool greys, white galleries | Machinists of North Umbermere / Historia |
| **Ochre Elf** | Elven, Lys-aligned | **Bright green eyes**, often **silver/pale hair** (Ludvig lock) | Green-gold Ochre / Tree of Life accents | Umber Elves who joined Lys |
| **Dragonborn** | Tall scaled humanoid | Snout, horns optional, breath-scar cues | Scale colors vary; Caldera Primordials lean primal | In Lys since Second Age openness |
| **Dwarf** | Shorter, stout | Broad features, thick hair/beard common | Stone, bronze, forge-practical gear | Lys-allowed peoples |
| **Gnome** | Small, nimble | Large curious eyes, lively expression | Bright trim on practical kit | Lys-allowed peoples |

**Usage:** Subclass gallery rotates ancestries so all seven appear. Race reference sheets are neutral adventurer pose (no class kit) for handouts.
