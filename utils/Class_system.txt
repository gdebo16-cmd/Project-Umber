Each class: preferred stat, armor proficiency, weapon proficiency, ≥3 subclasses, leveled abilities/traits.

Locked with Gabriel via Naman — 2026-09-16. Art assets by Artemis.

## Design notes
- Tier of Legends = levels **21–30** (epic).
- Level **20** and **30** often carry signature titles (listed below).
- Witch Green Magic ≠ Druid (CHA hexes vs WIS land/forms).
- Forge / Technomancer never explains the Forge Master.
- Hollowmancer is taboo in Lys.

## Roster

### 1. Warrior
- **Stat:** STR  
- **Armor:** All + shields  
- **Weapons:** Simple + martial  
- **Subclasses:** Vanguard, Weaponmaster, Warlord  
- **L30:** Apex Warrior  

### 2. Mage
- **Stat:** INT  
- **Armor:** None  
- **Weapons:** Staves, daggers, darts, slings  
- **Subclasses:** Evoker, Runeweaver, Scholar  
- **L30:** Apex Mage  

### 3. Witch
- **Stat:** CHA  
- **Armor:** Light (Witch Knight → medium + shields + martial melee)  
- **Weapons:** Simple + ritual blade  
- **Subclasses:** Hex Magic, Blood Magic, Fate Magic, Green Magic, Witch Knight  
- **L20:** Crone / Warlock · **L30:** Apex Witch  

### 4. Paladin
- **Stat:** CHA  
- **Armor:** All + shields  
- **Weapons:** Simple + martial  
- **Subclasses:** Oath of Ochre, Oath of Umber, Oath of Shadows, Oath of Starlight  
- **L30:** Arch Paladin  

### 5. Assassin
- **Stat:** DEX  
- **Armor:** Light  
- **Weapons:** Simple + finesse martial + hand crossbows  
- **Subclasses:** Shadowblade, Venom Bound, Ghost Walker  
- **L30:** Apex Assassin (Stroke of Luck → Apex package)  

### 6. Knight
- **Stat:** STR  
- **Armor:** Heavy + shields  
- **Weapons:** Martial melee + lance  
- **Subclasses:** Cavalier, Shieldbearer, Crown Guard  
- **L30:** Apex Knight  

### 7. Ranger
- **Stat:** DEX (WIS secondary / spells)  
- **Armor:** Light, medium + shields  
- **Weapons:** Simple + martial  
- **Subclasses:** Archer, Tamer, Stalker (dual-wield + deeper magic)  
- **L30:** Apex Ranger  

### 8. Druid
- **Stat:** WIS  
- **Armor:** Light, medium (non-metal) + shields (non-metal)  
- **Weapons:** Simple + scimitar, sickle, spear, staff, dart, sling, club  
- **Subclasses:** Circle of Roots, Circle of Beasts, Circle of Seasons  
- **L20:** Archdruid · **L30:** Apex Druid  

### 9. Berserker
- **Stat:** STR (CON secondary)  
- **Armor:** Light, medium (no heavy, no shields)  
- **Weapons:** Simple + martial melee (thrown ok)  
- **Subclasses:** Frenzy, Ironhide, Bloodwake  
- **L30:** Apex Predator  

### 10. Pugilist
- **Stat:** DEX (CON secondary)  
- **Armor:** None / padded  
- **Weapons:** Unarmed + simple melee + knuckles/cestus/gauntlets  
- **Subclasses:** Way of the Tiger, Way of the Bear, Way of Iron  
- **L30:** Grand Master  

### 11. Lancer
- **Stat:** STR  
- **Armor:** Medium, heavy  
- **Weapons:** Spears, pikes, glaives, halberds, lances, other polearms + simple melee  
- **Subclasses:** Phalanx, Skyreach, Twinstrike  
- **L30:** Child of Sparta  

### 12. Priest
- **Stat:** WIS  
- **Armor:** Light, medium + shields  
- **Weapons:** Simple  
- **Subclasses:** Lightbearer, Binder, Shadow Whisper  
- **L20:** High Priest · **L30:** Arch Priest  

### 13. Summoner
- **Stat:** CHA  
- **Armor:** Light  
- **Weapons:** Simple + focus implement  
- **Subclasses:** Necromancer, Hollowmancer, Technomancer  
- **L20:** Lich · **L30:** Arch Lich  

### 14. Songsmith
- **Stat:** CHA  
- **Armor:** Light + shields  
- **Weapons:** Simple + rapier, shortsword, hand crossbow + instruments (focus)  
- **Subclasses:** Warsong, Loresong, Diresong  
- **L20:** Maestro · **L30:** Renowned Songbird
